What is Self Chat?

Self Chat is a personal, chat-style space for your own notes, ideas, reminders and everyday thoughts.

Is it a messaging app for other people?

No. Self Chat is designed for messaging yourself, so you can capture thoughts in a familiar conversation format.

Does Self Chat work offline?

Yes. Self Chat is designed to work without an internet connection, and your content is stored locally on your device.
